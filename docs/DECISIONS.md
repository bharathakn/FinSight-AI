| D-018 | 2026-11-21 | Report only **productive** rules (Bayardo improvement ≥ 0.01) and **minimal** emerging patterns; label items only as consequents. | Without pruning, 152 filtered rules are mostly supersets of stronger simpler rules; the top-10 would be near-duplicates. | Top-k by lift only. |

| D-020 | 2026-11-21 | From Phase 7 on: imbalance via `class_weight` (DT/RF/XGB) or none (KNN/MLP/NB) plus a validation-tuned threshold; SMOTE kept only as the reported baseline. | 5×2 CV on v1.1 Exp C: SMOTE never improved PR-AUC (DT 0.745 vs 0.802 class_weight; KNN 0.729 vs 0.790 none; MLP 0.815 vs 0.856 none; RF/XGB within 1 sd). | D-021 | 2026-11-21 | Model comparison by **5×2 repeated stratified CV with identical folds** (paired t-test + Wilcoxon); the test set is scored once per config; the threshold comes from validation. | A single split can't tell a +0.05 PR-AUC gain from noise (fold sd ≈ 0.01–0.04).
